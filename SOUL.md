# SOUL.md — Dioscar Rodriguez Portfolio

## Purpose

This portfolio is a living introduction to Dioscar Rodriguez: a software engineer who turns complex enterprise systems into clear, durable, human-operable software — and who is exploring what changes when AI becomes part of the engineering system.

## Voice

- Direct, thoughtful, specific.
- Confident without inflated claims.
- Technical enough for engineers; legible enough for stakeholders.
- Favor verbs: modernize, map, migrate, design, connect, protect, ship.
- Explain the work through systems and outcomes, not buzzwords.
- Use first person sparingly and intentionally.

## Visual direction

- Primary surface: **Explore / Decide** — the page should feel like a field guide to a systems-minded builder, not a conventional résumé.
- Composition: editorial asymmetry, wide whitespace, oversized typography, interrupted grids, and strong left edges.
- Palette: warm paper, near-black ink, electric lime, tangerine, cyan. Purple is intentionally excluded.
- Texture: precise grid lines, small labels, thin rules, soft chromatic light, and subtle noise. Never default to glass cards or generic gradients.
- Motion: continuous but calm. Scroll should move the background field and reveal chapters; pointer light may respond gently. No motion should block reading.

## Content rules

- Preserve verified experience, technologies, employers, dates, education, and project details from the source portfolio.
- Do not invent clients, metrics, testimonials, titles, awards, or employment outcomes.
- Keep GlucoPlate AI as the featured public project.
- Make the distinction clear between current focus and past work.
- Links must be real and useful: GitHub, GlucoPlate AI, email, and the portfolio URL.

## Interaction rules

- Every major section has a meaningful anchor.
- Navigation stays lightweight and becomes useful on small screens.
- Interactive controls need visible focus states and at least 44px touch targets.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing transforms and looping animation while retaining the visual structure.
- The page must work without JavaScript; JS enhances the field, reveals, and pointer response only.

## Quality bar

The page should feel authored, not templated: fewer stronger elements, clear hierarchy, purposeful color, and enough negative space for the ideas to breathe. A visitor should understand what Dioscar does in the first screen and why the work matters before they reach the résumé details.
